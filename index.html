FAQ

Do my clients need to create an account?

No. Each client gets a private, unguessable link that expires. They answer and are done.

Do you connect to my QuickBooks or Xero?

Not at launch. You upload a CSV export, so ClientQ never needs access to your books. Direct sync is on the roadmap if early users ask for it.

What data do you store?

Only what's in the CSV you upload (date, description, amount) plus your clients' answers and receipts. You can delete a client's data or your whole account at any time. We don't sell data or use it to train AI models.

When will it be available?

We're building it now and opening access to the early-access list first. Joining is free and doesn't commit you to anything.

Is it a replacement for my practice-management software?

No — it does one job. It works alongside whatever you use today.
